Novidades

O que mudou.

Versão 1.0.1 (build 15). Disponível no Google Play. Em breve na App Store. Cada entrega é um commit público no GitHub.

Gerada a cada publicação, do histórico da main. As entregas aparecem em inglês, a língua dos commits.

28 de outubro de 2026

Android send: sign only the review the owner accepted

O que mudou

The review renews the plan a few seconds before it expires. When that happened while the passphrase sheet or the PIN was on screen, the core signed the new plan, with a fee or a send-all amount the owner had not seen. The screen now sends the review version it showed when the owner slid, and the core signs nothing if the version moved on: the review shows the new plan and asks for the slide again. iOS already signs the plan captured at the slide.

Found in the security review of Android 1008 (28/10/2026).

28 de outubro de 2026

Receipt alerts leave passphrase wallets out again

O que mudou

This reverts 4b5f68d on iOS and Android. The alert file opens after the first unlock, outside the encrypted wallet data, so with a passphrase wallet in it anyone who extracts the phone's data in that state finds the wallet the owner chose to hide. The wallet list itself needs the PIN; the alert file does not. The privacy policy and the security notes already said these wallets stay out, and now the code agrees. The trade-off is that a passphrase wallet does not notify when funds arrive.

Found in the security review of Android 1008 (28/10/2026).

Say self-custody instead of non-custodial; Play listing in lime

O que mudou

"Sem custodia" is not a term people know. The app, the site, the Play listing and the launch notes now say self-custody, in Portuguese and in English.

The Play listing takes the lime icon, a feature graphic made from the site's share image without the "coming soon" line, and the owner's seven lime campaign screens. play.py gains a production command that sends a bundle straight to review now that the app is published.

The site links the Google Play badge to the live app.

27 de outubro de 2026

Price chart follows a sideways slide; address check in character boxes

O que mudou
  • The price chart moves the point as soon as the finger slides sideways, without holding first. A vertical slide still scrolls the page.
  • Typing the hidden 6 characters of an address now uses one box per character, with the next one marked.
  • The "Address check" note on every send has no card around it, and its icon sits in the middle of the text.
27 de outubro de 2026

Bridge: opens on the stablecoin you hold most, with a centered status

O que mudou
  • The bridge opens on the stablecoin the wallet holds most across networks, from the network with the most of it to the second one (100 USDT on Arbitrum and 80 on BNB open Arbitrum to BNB in USDT). Once per wallet, and never after the user changes a network or coin.
  • The status after confirming is centered: the coin animation while it is on its way, the coin flip when it arrives, and a still figure with the destination faded when the deposit fails or comes back.
  • The review no longer shows the "you will sign 2 transactions" banner.

27 de outubro de 2026

Vault: a locked key is not a wrong PIN, and Forgot PIN waits 24 hours

O que mudou
  • A password key that is locked (device not yet unlocked) now reports "locked" instead of a failed PIN: the attempt is refunded and the counter does not move. Covers the PIN slot, biometry and Super Security.
  • Android vault format 2: the PIN key no longer carries the password in the AAD. Legacy items are read as before and migrated on a correct read. Invalidated keys are no longer deleted while probing; confirmation is required on biometric keys.
  • Forgot PIN asks first and erases only after 24 hours. Unlocking with the PIN or biometry cancels the request. A lost Super Security vault still erases at once. Same flow on iOS and Android.
27 de outubro de 2026

Voice: the challenge sheet hides from screen capture

O que mudou

The live transcript shows the voice phrase, a second secret. Voice is off in this version, but the sheet now gets the same capture guard as the other secret screens.

27 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase: the wait starts when the attempt is counted

O que mudou

The wait used to be armed only when a wrong passphrase was reported, so an attempt counted at the start but never reported (the app killed in between) raised the count without arming the wait. The wait is now armed together with the count, before the check; a correct passphrase clears everything and a refunded attempt takes its wait with it. iOS and Android.

27 de outubro de 2026

iOS: an unlock that started before the iPhone locked does not open the app

O que mudou

Every lock (and erasing everything) bumps an epoch; the unlock captures it before Argon2 and the Secure Enclave, and only opens if no lock happened in between. Before, the later open undid the lock from protectedDataWillBecomeUnavailable, and within the auto-lock tolerance the app came back unlocked without a PIN.

27 de outubro de 2026

Android: metadata writes reach the disk before returning

O que mudou

The metadata (passphrase attempt counts, nonce queue, sent addresses) was written with Foundation's atomic option, which renames without syncing the folder; on f2fs a forced restart could bring the old file back and undo a passphrase attempt. It now writes, syncs the file, renames and syncs the folder, like the vault files.

27 de outubro de 2026

Android: the core enforces the send steps, not only the screen

O que mudou

Review needs the destination lookup (tag and activation) and every check of the destination step; signing needs the review step, the destination lookup and the network confirmation of a first EVM, Sui or Aptos send. Going back can no longer jump forward.

27 de outubro de 2026

Android: locking always wins, and screen secrets go away with it

O que mudou
  • Every lock bumps an epoch in the core; an unlock that started before a lock (power button pressed during Argon2) no longer finishes with the screen off. The Kotlin side always asks the core to lock, and the trip to the background is recorded synchronously, so a destroyed activity cannot skip it.
  • Locking and erasing everything now clear the envelope seal, the envelope opening and the Super Security phrases, which only the closing screen used to clear. Words, envelope and phrases are kept only while the session is unlocked, checked under the same lock as locking.
  • Discarding an empty passphrase wallet without the PIN only accepts a wallet opened by this process, timed on the monotonic clock, on iOS and Android.
27 de outubro de 2026

Android: derivation vectors run on the Android binary before every bundle

O que mudou

Android replaces CommonCrypto, CryptoKit and Apple's NFKD with BoringSSL, swift-crypto and Foundation's NFKD. The BIP-39, BIP-32, SLIP-10, Icarus and per-network address vectors now run on the device with `testar-nucleo.sh --derivacao`, and `gerar-aab.sh` stops if they fail (ESCALIBUR_SEM_VETORES=1 skips and says so). 49 tests pass on the emulator.

27 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase wallets: the core refuses an empty passphrase

O que mudou

An empty passphrase would mark the regular wallet as a passphrase wallet that could neither send nor stop asking, since the signer refuses an empty one. The screens already prevented it; now both cores refuse it too.

27 de outubro de 2026

Sending: refuse your own address from another network with the same format

O que mudou

Sui and Aptos write addresses the same way, and a NEAR implicit account is the same hex without 0x, but each network derives the address differently. Pasting your own Aptos address on Sui sent everything to an address no key of yours controls. The send screen now refuses a destination that is the address of a wallet on this device on another of those networks, and a NEAR implicit account must be a valid Ed25519 point, so the hash of another network's address no longer becomes an ownerless account. iOS and Android, all 12 languages.

27 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase wallets: "never used" only when every network was actually read

O que mudou

The usage check counted a network it could not read as empty: the indexer that refuses the app (Base, Arbitrum, Polygon), the networks without history (X Layer, Sonic) and suspicious entries. The screen then told the owner who typed the right passphrase that it was a different one.

Now any history entry counts as movement, EVM networks also check the account nonce and native balance from two RPC providers that must agree (and token balances when there is no history), and a network that does not answer goes to the "did not answer" list. The text says which networks were read and that other apps may keep balances on paths Escalibur does not read, and the main button is "Use this wallet" instead of "Try another passphrase". iOS and Android, all 12 languages.

25 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase wallets: say when a passphrase opens a wallet that was never used

O que mudou

Every passphrase opens some wallet, so a typo used to open an empty wallet with no warning. After opening a passphrase wallet that is new on this device, the app now checks each network's history with the same readers as Activity and stops at the first movement. With movement, the wallet opens as before. With none, a new screen says no transfer ever happened on any network and offers to try another passphrase, which drops the empty wallet without asking for the PIN again (only for a passphrase wallet created in the last hour, never the last wallet), or to use it as a new wallet. The creation door's wait text no longer speaks of wrong passphrases. iOS and Android, all 12 languages, and a live network test (ESCALIBUR_REDE=1).

25 de outubro de 2026

Vault keys: an unknown answer never replaces a key

O que mudou

Android: the key that encrypts the vault files was silently regenerated whenever the Keystore did not return it. From then on no vault file opened, the right PIN looked wrong and the wallets on the phone were lost. It is now created only for an empty vault; otherwise the vault reports unavailable and the key is never replaced. A failed tag on an item without a password is now reported as damage, never as a wrong PIN.

Both apps: creating the device key refused only when the key was reported present, so an unknown answer let it be replaced. Any answer other than absent now refuses.

25 de outubro de 2026

Android secret field: emoji deletion, split surrogate pairs and physical keyboards

O que mudou

Deleting around the cursor counted UTF-16 units as characters, so deleting an emoji also took the character before it. A surrogate pair sent in two pieces is now joined, and an unpaired half is dropped instead of crashing the encoder. Keys from a physical keyboard now reach the field, with dead key accents. The on-device proof covers each case.

25 de outubro de 2026

New wallet: sealing an envelope right after creation is no longer forgotten

O que mudou

The created screen saved the wallet name from its own stale copy, which erased the envelope date the seal flow had just written. Both now write on top of the current metadata.

25 de outubro de 2026

Biometry: a key store that cannot answer no longer counts as a lost face

O que mudou

Only a biometric key the Secure Enclave or Keystore reports as absent drops the biometric slot and starts the 24 hour wait. An unknown state, such as a transient key store error, now refuses without erasing anything. Covered by a new vault test.

25 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase: an outcome that lands after the app locks now counts at the next unlock

O que mudou

When the app locked between counting an attempt and learning its result, the metadata was already gone, so the refund, the reset after a correct passphrase and the wait after a wrong one were silently dropped. Dropping the wait let someone who locks the app mid-check keep guessing without waiting. The outcome is now kept in memory and applied on the next unlock, on iOS and Android.

25 de outubro de 2026

Passphrase: a gentler count for opening or creating a wallet with a passphrase

O que mudou

This is where an owner who forgot the passphrase tries to remember it. The creation door now keeps its own count on the base wallet, separate from the base wallet's own doors: 10 free attempts, then a wait that starts at 1 minute and doubles up to 15 minutes, and a full day without attempts resets it. The day is measured on the monotonic clock of the same boot, so moving the device clock forward does not reset it. Sending, revealing and sealing keep the full ladder. Applies to iOS and Android.

25 de outubro de 2026

Hardened mode: a face or fingerprint that does not confirm no longer counts toward the wipe

O que mudou

Only a wrong PIN counts. When the biometric check fails after the PIN, the vault restores the attempts record and reports biometryNotConfirmed without counting or wiping. Settings, hardened mode and the Android screens now say so in all 12 languages, and two tests cover the rule.

25 de outubro de 2026

Removing a wallet never erases the app because the app locked mid-way

O que mudou

iOS: lock() swaps the metadata for an empty one. A removal that was waiting on the PIN or Face ID when the phone locked then saw no wallets left and erased everything: PIN, every other wallet and the settings. remove() now requires the unlocked session and the wallet in the list, and erases the app only after the list without it was saved.

Android: the "last wallet" decision is taken inside the save, under the lock, and the wallet must still be in the list there, so a lock between the save and the check cannot trigger the erase either.

Found by the bug hunt of 25/10/2026 (A1, B5).
